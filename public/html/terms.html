6. Orders

An order can only contain products from one Business. If you want to buy from two businesses, you must place two separate orders. This is because each Business fulfils its own orders under its own Business Rules.

When you place an order, the Marketplace will show you the total, the delivery method, the delivery fee (if any), and the Business Rules that apply. You confirm those details when you press Place Order.

A Business may accept, adjust, or cancel an order if a product is out of stock, if the delivery address is outside its delivery area, if the price was displayed incorrectly, or if the order appears fraudulent. If an order is cancelled by the Business before it is paid, any amount you have already paid is refunded.

Orders that are placed but not paid within the time window set by the Business (and, in any case, no later than 24 hours after the order is created) are automatically cancelled by the platform, and any reserved stock is released.

If you do not confirm receipt of an order after it has been delivered or picked up, the platform automatically marks it as completed 7 days after delivery, so the Business can close its books on the order. The data that makes up an order — items, amounts, delivery address, chat messages — and how long it is kept is described in Privacy Policy — Order data.

7. Delivery and pickup

Each Business sets its own delivery rules. Before you confirm an order, the Business's delivery options, delivery fees, delivery areas, delivery days, delivery time slots, and estimated delivery time are shown to you at checkout.

Depending on the Business, delivery may take any of these forms:

  • Delivery — the Business brings the order to the address or meeting point you gave.
  • Pickup — you collect the order from the Business's shop or from a pickup point the Business has named.
  • Chat to arrange — you and the Business agree the delivery or pickup arrangement in the order chat. Anything you agree in that chat becomes part of the order.

Delivery fees may be a fixed amount, a per-kilometre rate, a distance-based tier, or free of charge. A Business may also offer free delivery above a minimum order value. The applicable fee is always shown to you before you confirm the order, and it is the Business that sets it.

If the Business offers free delivery, the Business decides the conditions (for example, free within a certain area, or above a certain order value). If the Business does not offer delivery, only pickup or chat-to-arrange is available, and the platform will tell you so before you place the order.

Every order has a unique delivery code. This code is used by the Business and by you to confirm that the correct order was handed over at the correct moment. Keep the code until the order is in your hands.

The delivery address and any delivery coordinates you provide are used only to fulfil your order. See Privacy Policy — Location data.

8. Returns, replacements, refunds, cancellations

Each Business sets its own rules for returns, replacements, refunds, and cancellations. The rules that apply to your order are the ones configured by the Business you are buying from and shown to you at checkout, and they may differ from one Business to another on the same Marketplace.

The Business Rules that a Business can configure include:

  • Whether returns are accepted at all.
  • The number of days after delivery during which a return can be requested.
  • The condition a returned product must be in (for example, unopened, unused, with original packaging).
  • Whether a restocking fee applies, and if so, the percentage.
  • Who pays the return shipping (the Customer or the Business).
  • The number of hours after an order during which a replacement can be requested.
  • Whether replacements are allowed at all.
  • The number of hours after an order during which a cancellation can be made by the Customer.
  • Whether refunds are allowed, and if so, how they are processed.
  • Whether re-orders are allowed.

If the Customer requests a return, replacement, or refund, the request is submitted to the Business (and, where the platform is involved, to the BidhaaLink team) through the order chat. The Business reviews the request against its own Business Rules. If the Business approves the request, the Customer and the Business arrange the logistics.

If the Customer and the Business cannot agree, the Customer can ask BidhaaLink to review the matter. BidhaaLink may, but is not obliged to, act on the Customer's behalf to try to resolve the dispute. Where BidhaaLink does act, it will apply the Business Rules the Business set at the time of the order, and it will act in good faith.

Where a refund is approved, the refund is made to the original payment method where the payment rail supports it. Where the original payment method does not support a refund (for example, cash paid on delivery), the Business and the Customer arrange the refund directly. The record of the return or refund is kept as part of the order. See Privacy Policy — Order data.

9. Payments

Payments on BidhaaLink are processed through third-party payment providers. The methods currently supported are:

  • M-Pesa (Safaricom), including Paybill, Till, and Pochi la Biashara where the Business has configured them.
  • Airtel Money (Airtel), where the Business has enabled it.
  • PayPal, where the Business has enabled it.
  • Bank transfer, where the Business has enabled it and the Customer completes the transfer.
  • Payment on Delivery, where the Business has enabled it. In that case the Customer pays the delivery agent or the Business at the moment of delivery, and may be asked to accept a payment-on-delivery agreement before the order is placed.

The Business chooses which payment methods it accepts. The methods available for your order are shown to you at checkout. We do not hold customer funds on the Customer's behalf; when a payment is made, the funds go to the Business's configured account on the payment provider's rails.

If a payment is declined by the provider, or if the provider reverses a payment, the order will not be fulfilled and the Business will be notified. If you have paid and the order is not fulfilled, the amount is refunded through the same payment method where the provider supports it. The payment metadata we store, and how long we keep it, is in Privacy Policy — Payment data and Privacy Policy — Third-party services.

Prices, delivery fees, and any discounts are set by the Business. Prices are shown in Kenyan Shillings unless the payment provider settles in another currency, in which case the currency is shown at checkout.
