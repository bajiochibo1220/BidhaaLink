Step 1 of 5

Before you delete your business…

Please read exactly what will happen. This is not reversible once the 60-day grace period ends.

  • Your shop will be hidden from the marketplace immediately.
  • Your products will be hidden from customers.
  • You will not be able to log in during the 60-day grace period.
  • Past orders will remain visible to your customers for their records.
  • Your search tag will be held for 60 days and then released.
